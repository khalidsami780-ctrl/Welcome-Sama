# THE RED ROOM — الغرفة الحمراء
### An Interactive Botanical Experience for Sama (سما)

> *“مكان هادي وجميل، معمول ومستنيكي من بدري.”*  
> *“Somewhere between silence and beauty, a little garden was waiting for you.”*

---

## Concept & Vision
**THE RED ROOM** is an original interactive digital art experience created as a thoughtful, creative gift for Sama, celebrating her love for red roses.

Crafted in contemporary, natural Egyptian Arabic with genuine friendship, warmth, and lighthearted humor, it takes the visitor through five interactive acts of botanical transformation, dynamic ambient light, velvet rose petals, and a dedicated Spotify music corner for Amr Diab.

---

## The 5 Acts of the Journey

1. **Act I — The Beginning (البداية)**:  
   A solitary rosebud levitates in deep burgundy darkness, surrounded by glowing golden stardust and subtle cursor illumination. Features Sama's sculpted typography and the welcoming threshold.

2. **Act II — The Bloom (الوردة بتفتح)**:  
   The central rose awakens and unfolds in smooth botanical choreography. Features an interactive metamorphosis dial to scrub time and witness the blossoming of petals and golden stamens.

3. **Act III — The Living Garden (جنينة الورد)**:  
   An intimate constellation of six distinct red rose varieties:
   - *وردة الصحبة (The True Companion)*
   - *وردة الروقان (The Midnight Calm)*
   - *وردة الصبح (The Morning Light)*
   - *وردة الطاقة الإيجابية (The Bright Smile)*
   - *وردة الروح الحلوة (The Joyful Soul)*
   - *وردة سما (The Core of Sama)*  
   *Curiosity Reward*: Exploring the garden unveils a surprise 7th rose.

4. **Act IV — The Letter (الجواب)**:  
   A bespoke linen envelope sealed with an ornate crimson wax seal. Breaking the seal unfolds a heartfelt message of prayers, warm wishes, and the playful inside joke in natural Egyptian Arabic:
   > *"يارب أيامك تكون مليانة خير وراحة بال، وربنا يحقق لك كل الأحلام اللي نفسك فيها... وبالمناسبة، حاولي متتحوليش لسيد أكتر من كده 😂 خليكي سما وبس!"*

5. **Act V — The Constellation (نجوم سما)**:  
   A grand finale featuring tumbling silk petals, a monumental blooming rose, Sama's name illuminated among the stars, and a client-side **Keepsake Card Generator** that renders and downloads a 1200×1600 dedication art card.

## Technical Highlights
- **Vite + React 19 + TypeScript**: Blazing fast compilation and smooth 60fps rendering.
- **Generative Web Audio Synthesizer**: Pure Web Audio API ambient soundscape and glass harp chimes in F minor / D-flat major.
- **Physics Canvas Engine**: 3D tumbling rose petals with velocity repulsion, wind physics, and visibility-change auto-pause.
- **Bilingual & RTL**: Natural Egyptian Arabic and English with seamless language toggle.
- **Zero API Keys & Zero Third-Party Tracking**: Lightweight, privacy-respecting, and fully responsive across desktop (1440×900) and mobile (390×844).
